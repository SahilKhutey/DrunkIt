## 🆘 EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

### Server down
1. Check Vercel status
2. Rollback via dashboard
3. Post status update on Twitter
4. Resolve within 2 hours

### Critical bug
1. Acknowledge within 1 hour
2. Fix within 4 hours
3. Apologize + thank reporter

### Negative viral moment
1. Pause all scheduled posts
2. Respond calmly within 2 hours
3. Ship fix within 48 hours

### Burnout
1. STOP. Sleep.
2. Pre-scheduled content continues
3. Notify community
