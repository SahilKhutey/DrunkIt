# Commercialization And Ownership

DrunkIt / FACCP is proprietary commercial software owned by Sahil Khutey.

## Ownership Position

Sahil Khutey owns the repository contents, including the platform architecture, service implementations, documentation, workflows, policy models, tests, deployment assets, product direction, and commercialization rights, subject only to the separate licenses of third-party dependencies.

No public open-source license is granted.

## Restricted Uses

Unless Sahil Khutey gives explicit written permission, nobody may:

- Use the platform in production.
- Copy, modify, publish, distribute, sublicense, sell, resell, or white-label the platform.
- Host the platform for customers or third parties.
- Create a competing commercial service based on this repository.
- Use the repository contents to train models or generate derivative commercial systems.
- Remove or alter ownership, copyright, license, or attribution notices.

## Commercial Permission Model

Commercial access should be handled through a written agreement that defines:

- The permitted product, tenant, geography, and deployment scope.
- Whether the license is evaluation-only, internal-use, SaaS, white-label, reseller, or exclusive.
- Fees, revenue share, support obligations, and service-level expectations.
- Data protection, privacy, compliance, audit, payment, and regulated-commerce responsibilities.
- Source-code access, modification rights, escrow, and termination rules.

## Recommended Launch Review

Before commercial launch, complete review with qualified advisors for:

- Alcohol commerce licensing and jurisdiction-specific restrictions.
- Payment, refund, settlement, tax, and financial reporting obligations.
- Age verification, identity verification, privacy, and data retention obligations.
- Security controls, vulnerability management, penetration testing, and incident response.
- Terms of service, privacy policy, seller agreements, delivery partner agreements, and API marketplace agreements.

This document is a practical ownership and commercialization notice, not legal advice.
