# Global Independent, Craft & Local Alcohol Market Analysis (2026)

## Executive Summary

The global beverage-alcohol landscape entered a structural reset in 2025. Aggregate global consumption volumes contracted by **-2%** (~500 million 9-liter cases lost), yet value creation continues to migrate toward **local, authentic, craft, RTD, and premium segments**.

**Core Thesis:**
> The global commercial opportunity in alcohol is no longer driven by "more people drinking higher volumes." It is driven by **fewer, more selective drinking occasions + superior digital discovery + premium/local authenticity + new formats (RTDs / No-Low)**. DrunkIt's role is not mere delivery logistics; it is the **global discovery and commerce graph** for independent and emerging alcohol brands.

## 4. Consumer Behavioral Shift: Curation, Authenticity & Long-Tail Discovery

### 4.1 Gen Z & Millennial Selective Consumption
- **Participation**: 74% of Gen Z of legal drinking age participated in beverage alcohol in 2025 (up from 72% in 2023).
- **Curation Index**: Average categories consumed per drinking occasion fell from **2.8 to 1.8**.
- **Insight**: Consumers are not abstaining; they are prioritizing **occasion-specific, higher-quality, story-driven choices**.

### 4.2 The Indian Single Malt Phenomenon
- In 2025, Indian Single Malt (ISM) reached **~500,000 cases (+22% YoY)**.
- Domestic Indian craft single malt brands now capture **~92% of the category volume** in India, decisively outperforming traditional Scotch imports.
- Brands: *Amrut, Indri, Rampur, Paul John, Solan Gold, GianChand, Doaab, Crazy Cock, Aaghaz, Gamber Valley*.
