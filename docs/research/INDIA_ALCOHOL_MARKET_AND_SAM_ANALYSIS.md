# DrunkIt — Indian Alcohol Market Sizing, Regulatory Matrix & SAM/TAM Analysis (2026)

## Executive Summary

This document presents a rigorous, defensible, and regulation-aligned market sizing model for **DrunkIt (FACCP Platform)**. 

Prior naive estimates (e.g., claiming ₹10,000–₹20,000 Cr as immediate addressable delivery GMV) fail under investor and regulatory scrutiny because alcohol regulation in India is strictly a state subject under the Seventh Schedule (State List, Entry 8) of the Constitution of India.

**The Core Correction:**
> DrunkIt's Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) is defined by states and municipal jurisdictions where digital ordering, assisted commerce, or fulfillment can legally operate under the applicable State Excise framework—not simply by the gross consumption volume of alcohol.

## 3. Deep-Dive: West Bengal as the Primary Transactional Launchpad

West Bengal represents India's most mature, officially sanctioned digital alcohol commerce ecosystem.

### 3.1 Government eRetail Portal & eAbgari Data Points
- **Verified Buyers on Portal**: `152,301` registered consumers.
- **Completed Orders**: `192,811` placed orders.
- **Repeat Order Ratio**: ~`1.27` orders per registered user across initial cohorts.
- **End-to-End Digital Workflow**: Buyer KYC/registration → Geo-located licensed shop selection → Cart & price calculation → Digital escrow payment → Order push to licensed retailer → Delivery agent dispatch → OTP/QR verified home delivery.
- **Excise Scale**: West Bengal generated **₹17,907.65 Cr** actual state excise revenue in FY2024–25, with a budget estimate of **₹22,550.33 Cr** for FY2025–26.

### 3.2 West Bengal SAM Derivation
1. **Urban Off-Trade Premium Base**: Kolkata and urban West Bengal exhibit an estimated 12%–15% premium/luxury spirits consumption ratio (higher than the national 10% average).
2. **Addressable Premium Off-Trade Pool**: ~**₹1,500–₹2,500 Cr/year**.
3. **Digital Penetration & Propensity**: Assuming 20%–40% addressability through digital discovery and verified delivery:
   - *Conservative (20%)*: $₹1,500\text{ Cr} \times 0.20 = \mathbf{₹300\text{ Cr}}$
   - *Optimistic (40%)*: $₹2,500\text{ Cr} \times 0.40 = \mathbf{₹1,000\text{ Cr}}$
4. **Conclusion**: West Bengal Transactional SAM is **₹300–₹1,000 Cr/year**.

### 5.2 Order Volume Modeling for ₹100 Cr GMV Run-Rate

$$\text{Annual Orders} = \frac{\text{Target GMV}}{\text{Average Order Value (AOV)}}$$

- **At AOV = ₹2,500** (Standard Premium Spirits Basket: 1 Premium Whisky/Gin + Mixers):
  - $\text{Annual Orders} = \frac{₹100,00,00,000}{₹2,500} = 400,000 \text{ orders/year}$
  - $\text{Monthly Orders} \approx 33,333 \text{ orders/month}$
  - $\text{Daily Orders} \approx \mathbf{1,096 \text{ orders/day}}$ (Spread across 40–80 partner stores in Kolkata metro = 14–27 orders/store/day).

- **At AOV = ₹3,000** (Super-Premium / Single Malt Basket):
  - $\text{Annual Orders} = \frac{₹100,00,00,000}{₹3,000} = 333,333 \text{ orders/year}$
  - $\text{Daily Orders} \approx \mathbf{913 \text{ orders/day}}$
